## Substantive corrections

- Removed fixed calorie-burning estimates presented as typical effects, consumer dosing instructions, unsupported quotations, mechanistic certainty and blanket “good/excellent” safety labels.
- Distinguished short-term energy expenditure, fat oxidation and appetite endpoints from lasting weight or fat loss. The capsinoid trial is explicitly an older example with a limited population, not a complete verdict on all preparations.
- Corrected the 2024 ginger review's author attribution to Rafieipour and colleagues and made its evidence certainty explicit. Added the August 2026 diabetes-specific review, identifying the publisher's early accepted version and the narrower population.
- Added the 2026 berberine randomized trial and its null between-group weight result, with population and follow-up boundaries.
- Corrected the implication that green tea extract is established safe below the liver-risk threshold. Preserved uncertainty about bitter orange and causality of adverse-event reports.
- Removed the hardcoded reading time so the template calculates it. Kept five rendered research references and linked official safety guidance beside the relevant claims. Added a transparent revision note without claiming independent medical review.
