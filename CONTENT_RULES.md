# Editorial rules — Metabolic Science

Current policy, effective September 9, 2026. AGENT.md describes execution and config/editorial-schedule.json is the only active queue/calendar. Legacy calendars in other documents are historical and superseded.

## Editorial purpose

Serve the question the reader came to answer. Preserve and improve useful medical explainers; build an adjacent collection about meal organization, home exercise and evaluating resources. ClickBank monetization is through the existing Plant-Based Cookbook and FITin56 assessments when the reader's practical need matches. A paid resource need not be the best answer. Do not select a product solely from commission, Gravity or seller promises.

## Evidence and authorship

- Write clear English with a specific title, accurate description, one template-generated H1 and descriptive H2/H3 headings. Length follows the question; no minimum word/citation quota or repetitive padding.
- Use current primary sources. For medical statements use official labels, original studies or appropriate public-health guidance; distinguish population, dose, follow-up and limitations. Never invent a citation or quote. Explain uncertainty and do not give individualized dosing or treatment instructions.
- Author is editorial-team. Never invent a doctor, medical review, certification, testing, purchase, before/after story or product-specific efficacy evidence. Disclose AI assistance through the editorial policy. Show the original publishedAt; change updatedAt only after a substantive revision.
- Our product assessments describe public offer/checkout inspections; they are not hands-on tests. Report exactly what was checked, on what date, what remains unverified, price/currency, recurring billing, add-ons, cancellation, refund terms, audience fit and a free alternative. Vendor claims must stay attributed as claims.
- Avoid prescriptive claims that a cookbook, supplement or workout treats disease or guarantees weight loss. Ingredient/exercise research does not establish a named product's effectiveness.

## Links, layout and discoverability

- Each new article must have at least one relevant contextual incoming link and an index/hub entry. Preserve existing live URLs unless a documented consolidation justifies a redirect. Compare intent with the archive before drafting.
- Use canonical trailing-slash paths. Queries are only fixed campaign codes; never user input. Do not include sensitive search terms or calculator values in tracking.
- Paid links only through the shared AffiliateOffer component in disclosed product assessments, with sponsored nofollow noopener and no-referrer. Medical articles and calculators may link to useful educational guidance, but must not become product prescriptions.
- A guide can link to an assessment where it helps a decision; do not force unrelated offers into every page. Include useful free alternatives. No generic popup or site-wide affiliate banner.
- Price/check-out callouts are clearly commercial, keyboard accessible and readable on mobile. Verify table scrolling, reference anchors, canonical, social image and link destinations in the rendered export.

## Scheduling and validation

Three new pieces and two revisions per week during the four-week pilot. Titles in the queue are working hypotheses, not verified search demand. Refine within the same intent after research; update the queue if a duplicate is found. Do not publish future queued articles early or backdate missed work. Source-code and metadata migrations approved by the owner are not new daily article publications.

Run the operational, affiliate and export checks listed in AGENT.md. Deployment completion requires the matching public commit and page contents. New offers, price claims or changes to checkout behavior need fresh verification before publishing. Judge performance from observed funnel counts and ClickBank net outcomes, not impressions alone.
