**GLP-1 storage rules depend on the exact product and device.** “Keep it out for 21 days” is not a universal rule, even for every Mounjaro presentation. Match the brand, country, pen or vial type, and whether it has been used before relying on a storage limit.

This guide summarizes US injectable-product labeling checked September 15, 2026. The instructions supplied with your dispensed product take priority; ask a pharmacist when the carton and an online summary appear to disagree.

## Storage table by brand and presentation

Temperatures below are Celsius. Refrigeration at 2–8°C corresponds to 36–46°F; 30°C is 86°F. Do not freeze these products or use them after freezing. Keep them protected as directed in their packaging.

| Product and presentation | Refrigerated storage | Room-temperature or in-use limit |
|---|---|---|
| Mounjaro single-dose pen or vial | 2–8°C before expiry | Up to 30°C for a total of 21 days |
| Mounjaro multidose vial or KwikPen | Unopened: 2–8°C before expiry; opened: 2–8°C is permitted | Up to 30°C; discard at the earliest of 30 total days at room temperature, 30 days after first use, or four weekly doses |
| Zepbound single-dose pen or vial | 2–8°C before expiry | Up to 30°C for a total of 21 days |
| Zepbound multidose vial or KwikPen | Unopened: 2–8°C before expiry; opened: 2–8°C is permitted | Up to 30°C; discard at the earliest of 30 total days at room temperature, 30 days after first use, or four weekly doses |
| Ozempic multidose pen | Unused: 2–8°C; after first use: 2–8°C is permitted | After first use: 56 days at 15–30°C or refrigerated |
| Ozempic single-dose prefilled syringe | 2–8°C before expiry | 28 days at 8–30°C |
| Wegovy single-dose pen or prefilled syringe | 2–8°C before expiry | Before cap removal: 28 days at 8–30°C |
| Wegovy FlexTouch multidose pen | Before use: 2–8°C; after first use: 2–8°C is permitted | After first use: 56 days at 15–30°C or refrigerated |

Check the product-specific sections and instructions for use: [Mounjaro](https://pi.lilly.com/us/mounjaro-uspi.pdf), [Zepbound](https://pi.lilly.com/us/zepbound-uspi.pdf), [Ozempic](https://www.novo-pi.com/ozempic.pdf), [Wegovy](https://www.novo-pi.com/wegovy.pdf). The table does not cover oral Wegovy, compounded formulations or presentations labeled for other countries.

## How to read the table safely

An unopened product's room-temperature allowance is not necessarily the same as an opened device's usable life. Likewise, the date printed on the carton does not override an earlier in-use or room-temperature discard date.

For a multidose Mounjaro or Zepbound product, “30 days” is not permission to keep using residual liquid after the four weekly doses. For Ozempic, the 56-day multidose-pen rule does not apply to its single-dose syringe. Those distinctions are why identifying the presentation comes before counting days. [Lilly labels](https://pi.lilly.com/us/mounjaro-uspi.pdf); [Ozempic label](https://www.novo-pi.com/ozempic.pdf).

A simple record can prevent confusion:

- Product name and exact presentation from the carton.
- Date it first left refrigeration, with total room-temperature exposure where required.
- Date of first use for a multidose product.
- The discard date confirmed from that product's instructions.

Do not assume that returning a product to the refrigerator resets its room-temperature allowance. Follow the specific label's directions about refrigeration and cumulative time.

## What if a pen was left out or became too hot?

“Left out overnight” is not enough information to determine whether a medicine is usable. A cool room, a hot car and direct contact with a frozen pack are different exposures.

Before asking the pharmacist or manufacturer, collect the product name, device type, first-use date, estimated temperature range and length of exposure. Keep the carton or a photograph of its details available. A normal-looking solution alone cannot establish that an uncertain temperature exposure was acceptable.

If a product froze or exceeded its labeled limits, do not improvise a safety decision from storage advice for insulin or another GLP-1 medicine. Ask for product-specific guidance and replacement arrangements where needed. The manufacturer labels above are the reference for handling restrictions.

## Travel and everyday handling

Plan around the limits for the actual device you carry. Protect it from heat and freezing, retain its identifying packaging and check transport rules separately for your itinerary. A cooling container can be useful only if it keeps the medicine within the required range; direct freezing is not safer than room temperature.

Device handling also differs. Use the instructions provided for your pen or vial, including whether a new needle is needed and how the device is prepared. Do not borrow priming, dose-counting or needle-storage instructions from a different presentation.

Travel timing and storage are separate questions. If the injection day must change, consult the medicine's dose instructions.
