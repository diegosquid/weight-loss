Tirzepatide is the active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound. Understanding the ingredient, the brand and the population in a clinical study helps avoid misleading comparisons. This introduction uses US prescribing information and published trials checked on September 9, 2026.

## How tirzepatide works

Tirzepatide activates GIP and GLP-1 receptors. These pathways participate in glucose regulation; the medicine also delays gastric emptying. A description of its mechanism does not, by itself, prove that it is the best option for a particular person. See the [Mounjaro prescribing information](https://pi.lilly.com/us/mounjaro-uspi.pdf).

## Mounjaro and Zepbound have different labels

Mounjaro's US indications include glucose control in people aged 10 and older with type 2 diabetes. Its August 2026 label also includes reducing major cardiovascular events in adults with type 2 diabetes at high risk. [Mounjaro label](https://pi.lilly.com/us/mounjaro-uspi.pdf)

Zepbound has indications for long-term weight reduction in eligible adults and for moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity, alongside dietary and activity measures. The eligibility and dosing details belong to that product's label. [Zepbound prescribing information](https://pi.lilly.com/us/zepbound-uspi.pdf)

When reading an article or checking a prescription, ask which exact product and indication it discusses. An ingredient-level research result and an approved brand indication answer different questions.

## What comparisons with semaglutide tell us

SURPASS-2 studied adults with type 2 diabetes and compared tirzepatide with semaglutide 1 mg weekly over 40 weeks. Tirzepatide produced greater average reductions in HbA1c and body weight at the tested doses. The semaglutide comparison was not its 2.4 mg obesity regimen. [SURPASS-2 publication](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2107519)

SURMOUNT-5 instead studied adults with obesity without diabetes for 72 weeks, using higher tolerated doses of both medicines. It is relevant to a different clinical question. Read our [comparison of the two trials and their limitations](/glp-1/mounjaro-vs-ozempic/) before treating headlines as interchangeable.

## What to discuss before treatment

Tirzepatide labeling includes a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors in rats, with uncertain relevance to humans, and contraindications involving medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2. Common adverse effects include gastrointestinal symptoms. [Zepbound safety information](https://pi.lilly.com/us/zepbound-uspi.pdf)

Discuss your medical history, other medicines, pregnancy plans, contraception and any planned anesthesia with your prescriber. Ask which symptoms require prompt care and how to contact the treatment team. The complete medication guide provides safety details that an overview cannot replace.

## Prepare for the appointment

Write down the exact product being considered and your main treatment goal. Bring your medication list and ask how progress, tolerability and ongoing costs will be reviewed.
